FAQ

Questions we hear often

What's the typical timeline for a project?

Most web or mobile projects take 8–16 weeks from kickoff to launch, depending on scope. We'll give you a clear timeline after the discovery phase.

How do you price your projects?

We offer both fixed-price packages for well-defined projects and monthly retainers for ongoing work. You'll get a detailed quote before anything starts.

Do you sign NDAs and protect our IP?

Yes. We sign NDAs before any detailed discussion, and all code and IP created for you remains fully yours.

Which technologies do you specialize in?

React, Next.js, Node.js, Python, Flutter and major cloud platforms — but we choose the right tool for your project, not just what we know best.

Do you offer support after launch?

Every project includes a support window after launch, and we offer ongoing maintenance plans for teams who want long-term coverage.

Can you work with our in-house team?

Absolutely. We regularly work alongside in-house teams, either filling specific skill gaps or running a project end-to-end.

Have a project in mind?

Tell us what you're building. We'll get back to you within one business day with next steps.
