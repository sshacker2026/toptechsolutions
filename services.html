Our Services

Services built around your outcomes

Every engagement starts with a clear scope, a named team and a plan you can actually track. Here's exactly what each service includes.

01 / 06

Web App Development

From marketing sites to complex dashboards, we build web applications that are fast, accessible and easy to maintain.

Best for growing product teams
  • Custom web applications and internal tools
  • Progressive web apps (PWAs) and dashboards
  • API design, integrations and third-party services
  • Performance tuning, accessibility and SEO
  • Legacy codebase modernisation
02 / 06

Mobile App Development

Native performance where it matters, shared code where it saves time — apps that feel at home on iOS and Android.

Best for consumer & field apps
  • Native iOS (Swift) and Android (Kotlin) apps
  • Cross-platform apps with Flutter or React Native
  • Offline-first sync and push notifications
  • App Store and Play Store release management
  • Post-launch crash monitoring and updates
03 / 06

Cloud & DevOps

Infrastructure that scales with you, and a release process calm enough to ship on a Friday.

Best for scaling products
  • AWS, Azure and GCP infrastructure setup
  • CI/CD pipelines for fast, safe releases
  • Containerisation with Docker and Kubernetes
  • Monitoring, alerting and incident response
  • Cost optimisation and infrastructure audits
04 / 06

UI/UX Design

Design grounded in how people actually use your product, not just how it looks in a mockup.

Best for new products & redesigns
  • User research and journey mapping
  • Wireframes and interactive prototypes
  • Design systems built for engineering handoff
  • Usability testing with real users
  • Brand-aligned visual and interaction design
05 / 06

AI & Data Solutions

Machine learning and automation that solve a specific business problem — not AI for its own sake.

Best for data-heavy products
  • Predictive models and recommendation engines
  • LLM-powered chat, search and automation
  • Data pipelines, warehousing and dashboards
  • Model evaluation, monitoring and retraining
  • Responsible AI and data-privacy review
06 / 06

Enterprise & IT Consulting

An outside view on your architecture and roadmap, from people who've built systems at scale.

Best for established companies
  • Architecture and code quality audits
  • Legacy system modernisation planning
  • Technology roadmaps and vendor evaluation
  • Team structure and hiring advisory
  • Compliance and security readiness reviews
